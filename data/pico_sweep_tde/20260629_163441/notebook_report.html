NB12 — Pico Sweep TDE: Time-Delay Estimation via Phase Difference¶

Version: 1 Author: CB

1 Objective¶

Estimate the time delay $\hat{\tau}_{34}(f)$ between CH3 and CH4 over a wide frequency range (0.5 Hz – 200 kHz, log-spaced) from phase-difference measurements collected during Pico-driven single-frequency excitation runs. Results are cross-validated against the MLE estimator from NB11 (linear sweep, 5–100 kHz).

2 Data¶

Five runs in data/pico_sweep_tde/ collected on 2026-06-29. Each record contains per-channel amplitude and phase at the detected dominant frequency.

3 Methods¶

3.1 Phase-difference TDE estimator¶

For a single record at drive frequency $f_0$, the per-channel phase estimates $\phi_3^{(i)}$, $\phi_4^{(i)}$ are obtained from the FFT at the detected bin. The phase-difference TDE is:

$$\hat{\tau}^{(i)} = -\frac{\Delta\phi^{(i)}}{2\pi f_0}, \qquad \Delta\phi^{(i)} = \left[(\phi_3^{(i)} - \phi_4^{(i)}) + \pi\right] \bmod 2\pi - \pi$$

Wrapping to $(-\pi, \pi]$ constrains the unambiguous range to $|\hat{\tau}| < 1/(2f_0)$. At the lowest frequency analysed (500 Hz) this is ±1 ms; at 10 kHz: ±50 µs. Expected $|\hat{\tau}| < 5\,\mu\text{s}$ for the present setup, so no ambiguity.

3.2 Amplitude-weighted aggregation per band¶

For $N_b$ records at band $f_0$, the amplitude-weighted mean and standard deviation:

$$\bar{\tau}(f_0) = \frac{\sum_i w_i \hat{\tau}^{(i)}}{\sum_i w_i}, \qquad w_i = A_3^{(i)} \cdot A_4^{(i)}$$

$$\sigma_{\tau}(f_0) = \sqrt{\frac{\sum_i w_i (\hat{\tau}^{(i)} - \bar{\tau})^2}{\sum_i w_i}}$$

Weights are proportional to the geometric mean power of both channels, down-weighting low-SNR captures.

Interpretation:
  - τ ≈ 0 µs at all high-coherence bands → CH3 and CH4 equidistant from actuator.
  - Phase-diff TDE at 7.9 kHz (steel eigenmode): τ = +2.19 µs (N=47, σ=0.89 µs).
    This offset is reproducible and may reflect a real asymmetry or resonance-induced
    phase shift at the steel eigenfrequency (~9.17 kHz).
  - No systematic τ trend with frequency → no detectable wave dispersion in this range.
  - Wave speed lower bound: if d_sensor > 0.1 mm, then c_rod > d/|τ_max| ≈ 2 km/s,
    consistent with steel rod longitudinal velocity (≈ 5 km/s).

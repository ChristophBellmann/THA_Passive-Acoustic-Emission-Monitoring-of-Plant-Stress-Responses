# Experiments & Findings

The repository contains continuous measurements, targeted phase/time-delay work and watering-response experiments.

## Candidate around 3.8 kHz

A tracked feature around 3.8 kHz showed a reproducible day/night difference in CH3/CH4 coherence during the documented June 2026 measurement period.

This feature remains a **candidate**, not a confirmed plant emission. Its proximity to a building-related harmonic requires additional discrimination.

## Watering response

A documented watering event produced a strong narrow-band change near the tracked candidate frequency while the broader 0–5 kHz band changed much less. Subsequent watering experiments were structured with pre/post acquisition and explicit hypothesis tests.

These observations motivate further experiments but do not by themselves establish causality or biological origin.

## Rejected candidate

A feature near 6.75 kHz was identified as a harmonic associated with a building heating-pump artifact and is therefore treated as structural interference rather than plant activity.

Documenting rejected candidates is part of the project methodology.

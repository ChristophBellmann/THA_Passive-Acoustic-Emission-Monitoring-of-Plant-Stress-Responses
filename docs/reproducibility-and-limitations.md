# Reproducibility & Limitations

Reproducibility is a design objective of the project.

## Reproducible workflow

Acquisition and analysis are implemented as Python/Jupyter workflows. Continuous measurement includes status and heartbeat monitoring, while experiment outputs store structured metadata and analysis results.

## Important limitations

The current measurements are experimental and environment-specific. Important confounders include:

- mechanical coupling through pot, soil, supports and building structure,
- electromagnetic interference,
- sensor and amplifier transfer functions,
- environmental and diurnal changes,
- limited numbers of plants, sites and independent repetitions.

Reported candidate frequencies and responses should therefore not be generalized to plant species or stress states without additional controlled replication.

## Scientific status

The documentation distinguishes observation, hypothesis, artifact identification and validated result. This prevents exploratory findings from being presented as established biological effects.

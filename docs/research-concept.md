# Research Concept

The project investigates acoustic emissions from plants as a possible feedback variable for controlled-environment agriculture.

## Plant-in-the-Loop

A conventional environmental controller acts on measured variables such as temperature, humidity or substrate moisture. A Plant-in-the-Loop system adds a signal originating from the plant itself to the feedback path.

The research question is therefore not simply whether a sensor detects sound. The relevant questions are whether observed signals can be associated with plant state, separated from environmental artifacts, reproduced, and eventually converted into a useful control variable.

## Research strategy

The work combines literature review with experimental characterization. Candidate signals are tracked over time and tested against alternative explanations such as electromagnetic coupling, building services, structural vibration and changes caused by watering.

The project should be understood as ongoing experimental research. Observed correlations are not automatically evidence of a biological source.
